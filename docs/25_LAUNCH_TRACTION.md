# Launch and Traction Plan

## Track 03 implication

Monad explicitly says this track best fits teams that have grown a community, not only built one. A polished app with zero users is weaker than a polished app with a small, real, active test group.

## Target seed community

Do not recruit generic airdrop users.

Recruit:

- active crypto traders
- small trading-group members
- people who already post market theses on X/Telegram/Discord
- Monad ecosystem traders/builders

## Three-day acquisition plan

### Day 1

Prepare waitlist/tester form with:

- handle
- preferred contact
- markets traded
- current place they share ideas

Recruit first 10 likely testers while building.

### Day 2

Invite first wave after fork flow is stable.

Task for testers:

1. publish one real testnet thesis
2. fork someone else's thesis
3. leave one comment

Observe session live when possible.

### Day 3

Expand to 20-50 testers only if onboarding is stable.

Encourage one simple community event:

`Fork Friday` / `Monad thesis room`

Prompt:

- one root idea per market
- everyone chooses one idea to improve
- compare branch outcomes later

The event creates a natural tree instead of artificial seed data.

## Retention hooks in MVP

- visible forks on your ideas (notification/activity is P1)
- public reputation
- visible branch lineage and independently verified execution status; closed outcomes are optional later
- shareable thesis URL

Do not add points/token incentives during the hackathon. They attract low-quality behavior and dilute the product thesis.

## Interview questions

After use, ask:

1. Where do you share trade ideas today?
2. What made you choose to fork or not fork an idea?
3. Did the tree make attribution clearer?
4. Would you care about Originality/Contribution/Influence publicly?
5. What would make you return tomorrow?

Do not ask leading `Would you use this?` questions only.

## Evidence to collect

- number of real testers
- number of root theses
- forks
- unique external forkers
- verified executions
- 3 short quotes with permission
- one screenshot of a non-trivial tree

## Community-facing positioning

Use:

`Share the trade idea, not just the screenshot.`

`Fork a thesis. Trade your version.`

Avoid:

`Earn by copying alpha.`

That framing would push the product toward Track 01/copy trading and invite the wrong users.
